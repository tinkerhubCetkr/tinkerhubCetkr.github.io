Overview

./CodeConnect

"One step at a time. One punch at a time. One round at a time." — Creed (2015)

Objective :
To build a consistent problem-solving habit through collaborative learning, regular practice, and knowledge sharing, while also helping students understand the effectiveness of group study.

Program Overview :
./CodeConnect is a weekly online learning initiative where three programming problems will be shared every week. The problems will be selected from Codeforces, one of the most popular competitive programming platforms, where coding problems range from very easy to difficult based on their ratings. Initially, all participants will begin by solving beginner-friendly problems, and the difficulty level will gradually increase in the following weeks. The problems will cover a variety of programming concepts and topics, allowing participants to strengthen their overall problem-solving skills. Participants will solve each problem individually using their own logic and approach during the week, followed by a weekend online discussion where they will explain their solutions, logic, and approach. This allows participants to clarify their doubts, learn from each other, and understand different ways of solving the same problem. The program also encourages peer learning through discussions and idea sharing while motivating students to start and continue practicing programming regularly. .

Format :
* Every Week: Three programming problems will be shared.
* During the Week: Students solve the problems individually.
* Weekend: Online discussion and solution-sharing session.
